## Original materials attributed to Mohammad Ayati

The following original materials and product direction are attributed to Mohammad Ayati:

- The BookLand product concept and research-reading use case.
- The information architecture for a personal PDF library, reader, notes, highlights, and comparison workspace.
- The interaction ideas for page history, side-by-side reading, reading themes, full-screen reading, and study-oriented paper treatments.
- The product visual direction, including the calm library identity, paper themes, sketchbook texture, midnight reading themes, and app presentation.
- Original product descriptions, design decisions, and implementation direction created for this project.

The product-design record also includes the user-directed feature combination of comparing two different books or PDFs at the same time, independent page and theme settings for each reading pane, a returnable history of pages read, page thumbnails and index navigation, direct page jumping, separate notes and highlights, multi-line text highlighting with undo/removal, native text-selection actions, study paper themes, zoom and fullscreen reading, and importing PDFs from other applications through the share/open-in workflow. The full cross-platform record is maintained in the root [`INTELLECTUAL_PROPERTY.md`](../INTELLECTUAL_PROPERTY.md).
