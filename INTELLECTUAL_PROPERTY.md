## Original product ideas and feature combination

This record documents the product ideas, interaction concepts, and feature combination directed for BookLand by Mohammad Ayati:

- A personal PDF library that asks for a title when a PDF is imported and keeps the document available locally.
- Organizing books by editable titles, tags/topics, alphabetical order, date added, reading frequency, and last-opened or last-read information.
- Reading two books or two PDF files at the same time in a comparison workspace, with each pane able to open a different file and move to an independent page.
- Independent reading settings for the main reader and each comparison pane, including paper color, dark and study themes, sketchbook paper, reading mode, zoom, and visible page sidebar.
- A page-reading history trail that records the pages visited, shows the previous and next reading locations, and lets the reader return to an earlier page after jumping elsewhere.
- Page navigation through previous/next controls, direct page entry, page thumbnails, PDF index/outline navigation, and fullscreen reading controls.
- A separate notes and highlights area where annotations can be read without reopening the original book.
- Text selection, line-accurate multi-line highlighting, highlight colors, undo and removal of highlights, comments, and study notes.
- Preservation of native text actions such as Copy, Look Up, Share, Translate, and Services alongside BookLand’s annotation actions.
- Search and navigation designed for accurate research reading, including finding text in PDFs and comparing relevant pages across two documents.
- Smooth fit-to-page reading, zooming without unintended reset, page panning when zoomed, orientation-aware fullscreen reading, and controls positioned within device safe areas.
- Import from Files and from other applications through the iOS share/open-in workflow, including Telegram, Mail, Safari, and attachments.
- A local-first workflow in which the reader’s books, notes, highlights, titles, tags, and reading history remain on the user’s device.
- A document-tools dashboard for exporting a selected PDF page as JPG, splitting selected page ranges into a new PDF, combining multiple PDF files, and converting selectable PDF text into an editable DOCX.
- Reading Markdown files as selectable, formatted documents within the same local research library.
